# Screenshot footage

Screenshots show the real English Reel Lab interface using demo footage from **Sintel**. They do not show Timofey's private media library.

Film and related artwork: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), according to the project's [sharing page](https://durian.blender.org/sharing).

The source trailer was obtained from the [W3C media sample](https://media.w3.org/2010/05/sintel/trailer.mp4). For local verification, shorter silent clips were extracted and their baked black borders were cropped away. The screenshot compositions show those frames inside the application, including its portrait crop. Source video files and test exports are excluded from the repository.

The screenshot editor's soundtrack is **Dance field** by Centurion_of_war, released under CC0. See the [music credits](../../backend/src/reel_lab/assets/demo-music/CREDITS.md).
